01

What did my own app's prediction model actually do?

in progress · synthetic-data study complete

FemoraAI predicted each user's next period with a classic tracking method called a Kalman filter. I rebuilt it line by line and tested it on thousands of simulated users. It mostly worked, but one safety rule could make it stop learning for good.

Port of the production Java code, a steady-state analysis, and an exact Bayesian replacement scored with proper scoring rules.

9.1%of simulated users whose cycle changed were locked out by that rule
0.75 → 0.85how often the "80% likely" range really held, before and after the fix

All results use simulated data. The original model ran on real users.

02

How fast can a laptop simulate a fluid, and still be right?

in progress · three milestones complete

I wrote a program that simulates how fluids move, from scratch, for a laptop graphics card. First I proved it gives the right answer against known solutions. Then I found where its time went and made it much faster without changing a single digit of the result.

CUDA Navier–Stokes solver, multigrid pressure solve, mixed precision, shared-memory smoother, validated on the lid-driven cavity.
