01

Re-deriving a production cycle predictor

in progress · synthetic-data study complete

I shipped a per-user Kalman filter at FemoraAI, where it ran in production on real users' cycle data. Here I port it line by line, derive what it actually estimates, and test it against an exact Bayesian replacement.

Data note. Every result on this page comes from synthetic data, 2,000 simulated users with known ground truth. No FemoraAI user data is shown or used here.

0.2965steady-state Kalman gain. After about five cycles the filter is the same moving average it was blended with.
9.1%of users whose cycle level shifted were locked out by the 3σ outlier gate for their final eight cycles.
0.75 → 0.85empirical coverage of the nominal 80% interval, production against the robust model, under realistic contamination.
